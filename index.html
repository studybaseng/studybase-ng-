Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

On the Login page, enter your email and tap Forgot Password?. Check your email (and Spam folder) for the reset link.

All practice questions are free?

Yes. All JAMB and 100 Level practice questions are completely free. Premium is only required for Live Classes.

How do I join a Live Class?

Go to Live Classes → enter the invite code from your teacher. You need Premium (Student plan) to join.

Payment successful but still not Premium?

Sometimes it takes a few minutes. Close the app completely and open it again. If it still does not work, contact support with your payment receipt.

How many questions can I practice?

You can choose 10, 20, 30 or 50 questions per session in Timed CBT Mode. All subjects have many questions.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes, after the questions load once, you can practice offline. Progress is saved on your phone.
